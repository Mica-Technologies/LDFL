# FAQ

**Is this a port of a particular game?**
:   No. LDFL is *inspired by* the hero-shooter genre in general: roles, cooldown abilities, a
    charged ultimate. Every hero, ability, name, texture, model and sound is original.

**Do I need it on the server?**
:   Yes, on the server and every client. The server runs all the combat.

**Does it work in survival?**
:   Yes. Every item and block is craftable, recipes unlock in the recipe book as you gather
    ingredients, and there are advancements to follow. See [Items and recipes](../reference/items-and-recipes.md).

**Do the hero items wear out?**
:   No. None of them has durability.

**Can I be two heroes at once?**
:   No. You are the hero of the signature item in your main hand, or, if that holds none, the one
    in your off hand. See [Which hand counts](../guide/how-heroes-work.md#which-hand-counts).

**Can I use a sword with Bulwark?**
:   Yes. Keep the Bulwark Shield in your off hand and hold whatever you like in your main hand (as
    long as it is not another hero's item). You keep the barrier and the abilities.

**Does armor still work?**
:   Yes. Heroes wear armor like anyone else. The Tank passive adds on top of it.

**Do heroes work against bosses?**
:   They work against anything hostile. Wounded halves a boss's self-healing, Marked makes it take
    30% more damage, and Bedrock Anchor taunts it like any other mob. Bosses with their own
    knockback or targeting rules may resist the crowd control.

**Can I fight my friends?**
:   Turn on `combat.pvp` and split into scoreboard teams. See
    [Allies, enemies and PvP](../guide/allies-and-enemies.md).

**Is my progress saved?**
:   There is nothing to save. Your hero is whatever you hold; cooldowns and the ultimate meter
    reset when you log in. Advancements and recipe unlocks are saved like any other.

**Can I change the numbers?**
:   Every cooldown, damage value, duration and ultimate cost is in `config/ldfl.cfg`, and changes
    apply live. See [Configuration](../reference/config.md).

**Will there be more heroes?**
:   The hero system is built to grow. The [developer guide](../developer/adding-a-hero.md) shows
    how a hero is put together, if you want to try one yourself.

**Can I use LDFL in my modpack?**
:   Yes. LDFL is MIT licensed: include it, redistribute it, modify it. A link back is appreciated.
