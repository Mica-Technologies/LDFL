# Glossary

Absorption
:   Golden hearts on top of your health that soak damage first. Stone Skin and Ward Bell give it.

Ability
:   One of a hero's two cooldown abilities, on <span class="slot">R</span> and
    <span class="slot">G</span> by default.

Ally
:   Something your heals and buffs can reach: yourself, your pets, players on your team, and with
    PvP off every player. See [Allies, enemies and PvP](../guide/allies-and-enemies.md).

Barrier
:   Bulwark's projected wall. Blocks enemy shots and swings, lets allies through.

Cooldown
:   The time after using an ability before it can be used again. Shown on the HUD.

Enemy
:   Something your abilities can hurt: hostile mobs, and with PvP on, players not on your team.

Hero
:   What you become while holding a signature item. Gives a role passive and three abilities.

HUD
:   The hero panel in the top right of the screen. See [The hero HUD](../start/hud.md).

Hitscan
:   A shot that hits instantly along a line, with no projectile to dodge: the Longshot.

Marked
:   A status effect from Kestrel's Marker Dart: the target takes 30% more damage from everyone.

Mending Station
:   A floor pad that heals any hurt player who steps on it, then recharges.

Passive
:   The always-on bonus a hero's role gives: Tank toughness, Damage Wounds, Support regeneration.

PvP
:   Player versus player. Off by default; the `combat.pvp` config option turns it on.

Resource bar
:   The extra bar some heroes show on the HUD: Barrier, Anchor, Sentry, Vigil or Ward Bell.

Role
:   Tank, Damage or Support. Decides the passive and the HUD colour.

Sentry
:   Forgewright's automatic turret.

Signature item
:   An item that makes you a hero while you hold it. Each hero has one or two.

Taunt
:   Forcing mobs to target you. Monolith's Bedrock Anchor does it.

Ultimate
:   A hero's third and strongest ability, on <span class="slot ult">Z</span>, used when the
    ultimate meter is full.

Ultimate meter
:   Fills as you deal damage, heal and support. See [Ultimates](../guide/ultimates.md).

Wounded
:   A status effect from Damage heroes and the Bloom Flask: the target receives half healing.
